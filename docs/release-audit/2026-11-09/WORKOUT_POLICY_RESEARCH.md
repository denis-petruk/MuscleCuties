# Workout policy research — 2026-11-09

Scope: the rules touched by S-03 (readiness load step-down and cycle reps/extra set), S-04 (mid-session pain report),
S-05 (seven-day post-clearance window), E-06 (intensity label), readiness scoring, deload, HIIT 30-day validity,
experience promotion 20/130 and macro targets.

Method: primary sources only — position stands, peer-reviewed reviews and randomised trials, plus FAO/WHO and National
Academies documents for energy conversion factors. Vendor and blog sources were excluded. Each amendment below is a
**proposal** unless the row says it is already adopted in `docs/release-audit/2026-11-05/WORKOUT_POLICY.md`
(`MC-BETA-WORKOUT-1`). No clinical rule was invented, and no row recommends increasing load or exposure when data is
missing. Unknown never means "no".

## Owner decisions ratified (2026-11-09)

1. **S-03 / decision 4 load step:** Ratified Option A (amend policy to set-only volume reduction, reducing 1 optional set on low readiness / fatigue). Trainees naturally autoregulate absolute load via RIR. Planned prescriptions carry no load datum to step down from; no schema migration required. Matches production code.
2. **S-03 / decision 5 cycle dose:** Ratified Option A (amend decision 5 to symptoms/readiness only). A menstrual cycle estimate alone never changes reps, sets, or load. Symptoms and readiness drive volume adjustments; optional sets remain off unless recovery evidence is positive. Do not add cycle-specific rep/set overrides.
3. **S-04 pain surface:** Deferred for initial TestFlight (owner). In-session pain reporting and substitution will be introduced in the post-beta release.
4. **S-05 window:** Ratified Option A (delete the seven-day window, immediate restoration on clearance date: 0-day window). Cleared exercises return to normal prescribed work immediately on clearance day if eligible.
