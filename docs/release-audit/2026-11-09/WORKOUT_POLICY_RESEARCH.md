# Workout policy research — 2026-11-09

Scope: the rules touched by S-03 (readiness load step-down and cycle reps/extra set), S-04 (mid-session pain report),
S-05 (seven-day post-clearance window), E-06 (intensity label), readiness scoring, deload, HIIT 30-day validity,
experience promotion 20/130 and macro targets.

Method: primary sources only — position stands, peer-reviewed reviews and randomised trials, plus FAO/WHO and National
Academies documents for energy conversion factors. Vendor and blog sources were excluded. Each amendment below is a
**proposal** unless the row says it is already adopted in `docs/release-audit/2026-11-05/WORKOUT_POLICY.md`
(`MC-BETA-WORKOUT-1`). No clinical rule was invented, and no row recommends increasing load or exposure when data is
missing. Unknown never means "no".

| Decision 6 / S-04 — pain during a session | "Stop the provoking movement when pain is reported. ## Owner decisions requested (batch)

1. **S-03 / decision 4 load step.** Recommendation: implement the one-step reduction against the prescription source
   (starting weight or last actual), one decrement per session, baseline preserved. Alternative: amend the policy to a
   set-only reduction and keep the current implementation.
2. **S-03 / decision 5 cycle dose.** Recommendation: amend decision 5 to symptoms/readiness only; do not add cycle
   reps or sets, because current evidence does not support phase-based dosing and a second dose authority is a safety
   regression.
3. **S-04 pain surface.** Recommendation: inline action on the exercise card ("This hurts") that stops the movement,
   records the report through the existing injury store, and offers only reviewed substitutes. No new modal, no scoring.
4. **S-05 window.** Recommendation: delete the seven-day concept and restore an exercise only when current eligibility
   permits; the code/UX disagreement is worse than either option.

Until these are answered, the dependent items stay **BLOCKED** and no dose behaviour changes beyond what is already in
`MC-BETA-WORKOUT-1`.
