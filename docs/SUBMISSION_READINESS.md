## Public App Store review checklist

TestFlight acceptance alone does not complete a public App Store submission. Complete the gates above and then:

- [ ] Confirm app record, version/build selection, category, age-rating questionnaire, content rights, pricing, availability, applicable agreements, and storefront-specific requirements in App Store Connect.
- [ ] Supply accurate localized name, description, keywords, support URL, privacy URL, copyright, and screenshots of included functionality. Do not advertise pending or unaccepted features. Check [required metadata](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/required-localizable-and-editable-properties/).
- [ ] Complete App Privacy answers from verified data flows, including first-party server storage and applicable email processing. A required-reason manifest and App Privacy answers serve different purposes.
- [ ] Add review contact, purpose/audience, actual business model, external services, tested devices/iOS versions, and navigation steps for sign-in, consent, HealthKit, and deletion. Supply a working synthetic demo account through App Store Connect's protected fields; do not commit credentials or one-time codes. Ensure reviewers can enter without depending on a private mailbox. See [Provide information for a complete review](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/complete-review).
- [ ] Verify wellness wording and cycle/fasting claims against accepted scope and qualified review. Do not claim unverified medical capabilities or infrastructure guarantees.
- [ ] Keep the reviewed backend and access available throughout review; record the intended App Store release option.
